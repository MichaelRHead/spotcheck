# Privacy Policy for SpotCheck: Media Detective

**Last Updated:** October 2026

**SpotCheck: Media Detective** ("SpotCheck", "we", "our", or "the App") is an educational media literacy application designed for pre-teens, middle school students, families, and educators. We are committed to protecting user privacy and complying with applicable child privacy regulations, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy Requirements.

## 1. Information We Collect
**SpotCheck does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information or device identifiers.**
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address, name, or phone number to use the App.
* **No Analytics or Tracking:** The App does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or behavioral tracking scripts.
* **No Device Permissions:** The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or photo library.

## 2. Local Data Storage
All learning progress—including solved media cases, Detective XP, streak counts, and unlocked badges—is stored strictly locally on your device using Android's on-device Room database. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings or uninstalling the App.

## 3. Simulated Social Content
All video scenarios, creator profiles, comments, and source checks inside SpotCheck are pre-scripted, offline educational simulations created specifically to teach media literacy. The App does not connect to external social media networks or allow user-to-user chat or public posting.

## 4. Third-Party Services & Advertising
SpotCheck contains **no third-party advertisements** and **no in-app purchases**.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If we update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version with an updated effective date.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's privacy practices, please contact the developer through the support email listed on the Google Play Store listing page.
